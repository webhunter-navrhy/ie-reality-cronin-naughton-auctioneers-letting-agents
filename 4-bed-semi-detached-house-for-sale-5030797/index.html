About the property

Description property

Cronin Naughton Auctioneers are delighted to present 23 Monastery Terrace, Blarney Road to the market.

It is a spacious four-bedroom semi-detached home ideally located in the popular Monastery Terrace development on Blarney Road. The property offers well-proportioned accommodation, generous outdoor space and excellent connectivity to Cork City and surrounding employment hubs.

On entering the property, the accommodation includes a spacious living room, providing a comfortable main reception space, while the fully fitted kitchen offers excellent functionality for everyday family living.

The dining room provides valuable additional flexibility and could easily be adapted for use as a playroom, family room or home office, depending on the needs of the new owner. Patio doors lead directly from this area to a generous patio and large, secure rear garden, providing an excellent outdoor space for children, entertaining or relaxing. A side entrance adds further convenience and practicality.

Upstairs, the property offers four bedrooms, including a bedroom with its own ensuite, together with a main family bathroom. A further downstairs WC completes the accommodation.

Externally, the property benefits from off-road parking to the front, a side entrance and a substantial, secure rear garden.

Excellent Location

The location is a particular feature of this property. Situated on Blarney Road, Monastery Terrace provides convenient access to Cork City Centre, Blarney and Ballincollig, making it an attractive choice for families and commuters alike.

The property is particularly well positioned for access to major local employers, including Apple's Cork campus, while Cork city centre is readily accessible.

The area is also well served by public transport, with the 202 bus route providing connections towards Apple, Cork City Centre and other parts of the city.

There is an excellent selection of educational facilities in the surrounding area. Nearby schools include Blarney Street CBS, St. Mary's on the Hill National School, Scoil Padre Pio National School, St Joseph's National School and St Fin Barre's National School. Post-primary options include Terence MacSwiney Community College, Presentation College Mardyke, St Aloysius School, North Monastery Secondary School and Gaelcholáiste Mhuire. Several of these are within approximately 0.5–1.7km of properties on Monastery Terrace, based on local property/location data.
